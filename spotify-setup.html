Connect Spotify

Play on the Spotify app (phone or PC). You do not need to leave this site open for others to see what you’re playing. Closing the browser tab stops site audio (normal browser rule) — Spotify itself keeps playing.

  1. Open Spotify Developer Dashboard → your app (or Create app).
  2. Settings → Redirect URI exactly:
    https://slits.lol/api/spotify/callback
  3. Paste Client ID + Secret → click Connect Spotify → Approve.

Open site

If credentials were saved before, leave the fields filled (or paste again) and click Connect — it clears the old login and opens Spotify approval.

Checking…

After connect, play something on Spotify. The site updates in about a second (click enter for audio).
